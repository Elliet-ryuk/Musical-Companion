# Musical Scales

## What Is a Scale?

A scale is an ordered sequence of musical notes arranged according to a particular pattern.

Scales are important because they provide the collection of notes used to build melodies, chords, and musical ideas.

## Major Scale

The major scale is one of the most commonly used scales in Western music.

Its interval pattern is:

Whole - Whole - Half - Whole - Whole - Whole - Half

For example, the C major scale is:

C - D - E - F - G - A - B - C

The C major scale contains no sharps or flats.

## Minor Scale

The natural minor scale has the following interval pattern:

Whole - Half - Whole - Whole - Half - Whole - Whole

For example, the A natural minor scale is:

A - B - C - D - E - F - G - A

## Pentatonic Scale

A pentatonic scale contains five different notes.

The minor pentatonic scale is especially useful for guitarists and improvisation.

The A minor pentatonic scale contains:

A - C - D - E - G - A

## Chromatic Scale

The chromatic scale contains all twelve notes used in Western music.

Starting from C:

C - C# - D - D# - E - F - F# - G - G# - A - A# - B - C

## Scale Degrees

Each note in a scale has a number called its scale degree.

For the C major scale:

1. C
2. D
3. E
4. F
5. G
6. A
7. B

The first degree is called the tonic.

## Tonic

The tonic is the first note of a scale and often feels like the tonal center or point of resolution.

For example, C is the tonic of the C major scale.

## Why Scales Matter

Learning scales can help musicians:

- Understand melodies
- Build chords
- Improvise
- Practice technique
- Understand musical keys
- Create musical ideas
