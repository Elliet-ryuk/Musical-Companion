# Music Theory Fundamentals

## What Is Music Theory?

Music theory is the study of the basic principles used to understand how music is organized. It helps musicians understand notes, scales, chords, rhythm, melody, and harmony.

## Musical Notes

The basic musical notes are:

- A
- B
- C
- D
- E
- F
- G

After G, the note names repeat.

On a piano, the notes are arranged in a repeating pattern. On guitar, the same musical notes can appear at different positions on the fretboard.

## Octave

An octave is the distance between one note and another note with the same name that is higher or lower.

For example:

C → C

The second C is one octave higher.

## Semitone

A semitone is the smallest commonly used distance between two notes in Western music.

Examples:

- C → C#
- E → F
- B → C

Notice that E-F and B-C do not have a black key between them on a piano.

## Whole Tone

A whole tone consists of two semitones.

For example:

C → D

The distance from C to D is a whole tone.

## Scale

A scale is a sequence of notes arranged in ascending or descending order.

One common example is the C major scale:

C - D - E - F - G - A - B - C

## Chord

A chord is a group of notes played together.

For example, a C major chord contains:

C - E - G

## Melody

A melody is a sequence of musical notes that forms a recognizable musical idea.

## Harmony

Harmony describes how different notes or chords sound together.

For example, when several chords are played underneath a melody, they create harmonic support for that melody.

## Rhythm

Rhythm describes the timing and duration of sounds and rests in music.

Important rhythmic concepts include:

- Beat
- Tempo
- Meter
- Note duration
- Rest

## Tempo

Tempo describes how fast or slow music is performed.

Tempo is commonly measured in BPM, meaning beats per minute.

For example:

60 BPM = approximately one beat per second.

## Why Music Theory Is Useful

Music theory can help musicians:

- Understand how songs are constructed
- Learn chords and scales
- Understand relationships between notes
- Improve improvisation
- Write and arrange music
- Communicate musical ideas with other musicians
